This is an update and rework of the mod Natures Pretty Sweet. Split up into multiple mods for convenience.
Originally created by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1211694919]tkkntkkns[/url]
Then continued by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3542949511]Mlie[/url]

When I first found this mod a year ago I fell in love with it. Downloaded it, opened up a quicktest, and watched the terrain slowly transform from the usual dryness to a density that made the dense forest feel like a forest when it rained.

The overall performance of the mod was not great. So I embarked on a multi-month journey to rewrite and optimize the heck out of this mod. Many months and a couple mental breaks later, I think it's ready for release.
This mod has been split into three parts, [b]Weather Effects[/b], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838151757]Biomes[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3838153201]Snow textures[/url].
[h1]Map features[/h1]These can all be turned off in settings.
[list][*]Rain effects - Soil and sand will darken when wet. This results in an increase in soil fertility. Other terrain, like mud or river banks, can completely change to shallow water.
[*]Oceans tides - Realistic tides following a semidiurnal cycle. You can also find tiles that contain diurnal and mixed semidiurnal cycles.
[*]River levels - Rivers will rise and fall during the year. This can depend on rainfall, season, and different events.
[*]Rare items - Ocean tides can bring in items. This ranges from seaweed or wood logs, to rare treasure like persona cores or skilltrainers. They will wash away during the next tide.
[*]Frost grid - When it gets cold, frost will appear around the map. This is a subtle effect that is purely cosmetic.
[*]Seasonal weather - Most biomes have weather tuned to the current season/month. Expect more rain in the spring than in the summer for example.
[*]Seasonal incidents - Heat waves can be more common in the summer and less common in the winter.
[*]Seasonal diseases - You are less likely to get a cold in the summer and more likely in the fall and winter.
[*]Hot Springs and Cold Springs can appear on tiles. Hot springs appear in colder areas while cold springs appear in warmer. Pawns resting in these springs will gain a mood boost. They can enter springs naturally to relax, or even remove heatstroke in cold springs.
[*]New weather - Wind storms, overcast, thick fog, dust storms.
[*]New incidents - herd migrations, droughts, wildflower blooms.
[/list][h1]Pawn Features[/h1]These can all be turned off in settings. There is also an option to affect all pawns, or only colonists.
[list][*]Water impact - Pawns will be affected more by water. Rain or entering water will affect a pawns comfortable temperature range. They will be more comfortable in hotter weather but less comfortable in cold.
[*]Drowning - If downed, pawns can drown in water.
[*]Paths - In high traffic areas, pawns can generate dirt paths from regular paths. Pawns will also pack down snow naturally while moving.
[*]More noticeable cold breath. Pawns emit a much more noticeable breath of air when it's cold
[/list][h1]Additional features[/h1]These options are similar to features already present in Odyssey. As such they will not be active if Odyssey is active. They do not make use of any Odyssey features.
[list][*]Water freezes - This uses a custom mechanic made years before Odyssey to make water freeze when it's cold. As such, it is only available if Odyssey is not loaded.
[*]"swimming" - Pawms can "swim" while in water. This is purely visual.
[/list][h1]Performance[/h1]My main motivation for making this was improving performance. As such, I did all I could to make this much more performant than previous versions. The default settings are what I found comfortable while testing on my Ryzen 7 2700(RIP). Some settings can be heavier than others. Check the impact and turn off what you don't want on. If you do have issues, measure the performance in the Frame Profiler or Dubs Analyzer and let me know of any excessive slowdowns you observe. Ping me in the general discord, dubs discord, or wherever you find me. Additionally, warnings will pop up if you have any mods patching methods this mod makes heavy use of. More info below.
[h1]Compatibility[/h1]For mod compatibility please see https://github.com/Halicade/NPSWeatherEffects/blob/Main/ModCompatibility.md If a mod is not listed, it either means that it is compatible, or it was not tested with it.
This might be save compatible with the original Natures pretty sweet. However, I did not aim to make compatibility. You will require both this and the NPS: Biomes mod if you choose to swap. Make a copy of your save first in case. You will also want to turn off/revert all currently active effects. No frozen or wet terrain for example. 
There are some warnings that pop up in the logs at startup if certain settings might be affected.
Terraforming mods should be compatible. However, if a wet terrain block is terraformed, it may not be affected until save and reload.
This might be save compatible. As with any mod that adds items/terrain hash collisions are possible. While I don't recommend removal, there is an option to disable all weather effects and will remove all effects from the current map.
[h1]Q&A[/h1][list][*][b]I turned off a setting but the effects are still there![/b] Some effects will take a couple seconds to remove themselves. For other effects, there is an option "Reset map" which will reapply all settings on the current map.
[*][b]Can you add compatibility with x[/b] Send me a link, let me know what needs compatibility with it, and I'll see what I can do.
[*][b]How can I add compatibility with my mod[/b] There is a "AddingCompatibility" folder in the github. That should explain how to add compatibility. You can do it from your side or send me a pull request. If you have questions or something isn't clear, let me know.
